How to Choose the Right Frame Shape for Your Face

Choosing the right spectacle frame shape depends on your face shape. The goal is balance — angular frames soften round faces, and rounded frames soften angular faces.

How to Measure Your Frame Size

Frame size is written as lens width × bridge width × temple length (in mm). Check the inner temple arm of your current glasses — the numbers are printed there.

  • Lens width: 43–58mm — the horizontal width of one lens
  • Bridge width: 15–22mm — the nose piece between the two lenses
  • Temple length: 130–150mm — the arm that goes over your ear (determines overall frame width on your face)

Temple length is important — it determines how the frame sits on your face width. Longer temples accommodate wider faces and ensure the arms reach comfortably behind your ears.
