How to Read Your Eye Prescription

Understanding every value on your prescription for ordering glasses online

Sample Prescription

SPH CYL AXIS ADD
OD (Right) -2.50 -0.75 180 +1.50
OS (Left) -3.00 -1.00 175 +1.50

PD: 64mm

SPH (Sphere)

The main correction power. This is the most important value on your prescription.

  • Negative (e.g. -2.50) → corrects myopia (can't see far clearly)
  • Positive (e.g. +1.50) → corrects hyperopia (can't see near clearly)
  • 0.00 / Plano / Pl → no spherical correction needed

Values come in 0.25 increments (e.g. -1.25, -1.50, -1.75). SPH is required for both eyes when ordering at Optiwar.

High prescription? If your SPH exceeds ±6.00, we recommend Extra-Thin lenses (1.67 index) for thinner, lighter glasses.

CYL (Cylinder)

Corrects astigmatism — where your cornea is shaped more like a rugby ball than a perfect sphere, causing blurred or distorted vision at all distances.

  • If your prescription has no CYL, you don't have astigmatism — leave it blank when ordering
  • If CYL is present, you must also have AXIS
  • Common values: -0.25 to -4.00

AXIS

The angle of astigmatism correction, measured in degrees from 0 to 180.

  • Only meaningful when CYL is present
  • If CYL is blank, AXIS should be blank too
  • If CYL is present but AXIS is missing on your prescription, contact your eye doctor

ADD (Addition)

Extra magnification for reading/close work. Prescribed for presbyopia — the age-related difficulty focusing on near objects, typically starting after age 40.

  • Range: +0.50 to +3.50 (higher = more near magnification)
  • Usually same for both eyes
  • Required when ordering bifocal or progressive lenses
  • If your prescription has ADD but you're ordering single-vision, you'll only get distance correction

Prism (uncommon)

Corrects eye alignment issues (strabismus / binocular vision problems). Most prescriptions do not include prism.

  • Measured in prism dioptres with a base direction: Base Up (BU), Base Down (BD), Base In (BI), Base Out (BO)
  • If your prescription includes prism, upload the prescription image when ordering so our team can verify

Eye Notation

Abbreviation Meaning Also written as
OD Right eye (Oculus Dexter) R, RE, Right
OS Left eye (Oculus Sinister) L, LE, Left
OU Both eyes (Oculus Uterque) —

Quick Validation Checklist

  • SPH present for both eyes? Required.
  • CYL present? → AXIS must also be present (0-180).
  • AXIS present but no CYL? → Something's wrong — check your prescription.
  • Ordering bifocal/progressive? → ADD power is required.
  • SPH values in 0.25 increments? (e.g. -1.25, not -1.30)
  • PD between 54-74mm for adults?
