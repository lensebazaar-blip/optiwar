Hello{% if name %} {{ name }}{% endif %} — one photo, and we can size your frames

It takes about a minute. Please read step 2: without it we cannot work out your measurements.

  1. Face the camera straight on, in good light, with your eyes open and hair off your face.
  2. Hold a bank or credit card flat under your eyes, against your cheeks, numbers facing the camera.
  3. Hold the phone at arm's length, at eye level, and take the photo.

Why the card? Every bank card is exactly the same size worldwide, so it tells us the real scale of the photo. Without something of known size in the picture, a photo cannot give millimetres — and a guessed measurement means glasses that do not sit correctly.

The photo is used only to measure your pupillary distance and face width for your order. It is stored securely, is not published anywhere, and is deleted after {{ retention_days }} days.
